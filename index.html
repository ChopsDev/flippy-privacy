Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Flippy app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by NahImGood (collectively, the "Application"). NahImGood is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

Registration is not required. The Application shows ads using Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the Application automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage
  • Device and account identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID, app set ID, and, if applicable, identifiers related to signed-in accounts on your device

This data is encrypted in transit. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not collect precise information about the location of your mobile device. Your IP address may be used to estimate your device's general location, as described above.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

Yes. The data listed above is collected and shared through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which is provided by Google.

What are my opt-out rights?

You can reset or delete your advertising ID using the ad ID controls in your Android settings menu. You can stop further data collection by the Application by uninstalling it.
